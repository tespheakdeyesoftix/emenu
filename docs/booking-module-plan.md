# eMenu Booking Module Plan

Guests can request a table at a specific branch. Restaurant staff receive a Telegram alert and can review the request in Admin Control. Super Control controls which subscription plans, businesses, and branches offer Booking. This implementation plan reflects all 20 decisions from the booking interview.

## Confirmed booking requirements

Add a public booking page at `/booking?branch=<branch-slug>` on the business's eMenu domain. The form includes arrival date, arrival time, number of guests (pax), booking preference, and a note. Guests check the terms and conditions box before confirming. Save each valid submission in a new `bookings` table and send a Telegram alert to the chat configured under **Super Control → Business Detail → Booking Settings**.

Add a **Booking** switch for each business in Super Control Feature Settings and a **Booking** switch on each subscription plan. In Admin Control, add a **Bookings** page with a list, a calendar, and booking details.

## Booking goal decisions

20 of 20 questions answered.

1. **Booking confirmation.** Staff check availability before confirming a request.
2. **Guest contact details.** The booking form requires the guest's name and phone number.
3. **Guest follow-up.** Staff call the guest to confirm or decline the request.
4. **Links without a branch.** Ask the guest to select a branch before opening the booking form.
5. **Booking preference.** Offer preset choices and a free text field for preference details.
6. **Booking schedule.** Follow regular business hours and allow specific dates to be blocked.
7. **Arrival slots.** Guests choose from fixed slots. Super Control sets the interval between slots in Booking Settings.
8. **Booking window.** Super Control can set the minimum notice and maximum advance booking window in Booking Settings. Default to at least two hours' notice and up to 30 calendar days ahead.
9. **Party size.** Super Control can set the minimum and maximum number of guests per booking. Default to 1–12 guests.
10. **Slot capacity.** Accept multiple valid requests for the same slot. Staff check capacity before confirming each request.
11. **Booking terms and languages.** Each business supplies its own terms in Khmer and English. The guest form and terms support both languages.
12. **Enablement.** Booking requires an assigned subscription plan that includes it and an enabled business switch in Super Control.
13. **Access when disabled.** If either switch is off, authorized staff can still view existing bookings in read-only mode in Admin Control.
14. **Branch participation.** An active branch accepts booking requests only after Super Control enables it individually in Booking Settings.
15. **Admin Control access.** Business owners, managers, and staff explicitly granted Booking access can view bookings and guest contact details for their business.
16. **Booking status changes.** Owners and managers can confirm, decline, or cancel bookings in Admin Control while Booking is enabled. A staff Booking grant gives read access.
17. **Calendar default.** The Admin Control booking calendar opens in month view.
18. **Telegram destination.** Configure the Telegram Chat ID for each branch in Super Control Booking Settings. Route each booking alert to its branch's configured chat.
19. **Telegram bot.** Configure a dedicated booking bot per business in Super Control Booking Settings. It sends alerts to the chat configured for each booking's branch.
20. **Telegram alert contents.** Include the booking summary, guest name, phone number, preset and free text preferences, and the separate booking note.

## Guest and staff flow

The guest submits a booking request. Staff check availability and call the guest with their decision.

1. The guest opens `/booking?branch=<branch-slug>` on a business's custom menu domain. On the shared menu domain, use `/<business-slug>/booking?branch=<branch-slug>` because multiple businesses can use the same branch slug, such as `main`. If the link omits `branch`, ask the guest to choose from that business's active branches that accept bookings. Add the selected branch to the URL and show the business and branch names above the form. If no branches accept bookings, the specified branch is invalid or inactive, or bookings are disabled, show an unavailable message and prevent submission.
2. The guest picks an arrival date and one of the fixed time slots within regular business hours in the restaurant's time zone. Booking Settings determines the slot interval, and the form excludes blocked dates and slots outside the booking window. The guest enters the party size within the configured limits, can pick a preset booking preference, and can add preference details and a separate note. Require the guest's name and phone number so staff can follow up. Display the form and the business's terms in Khmer or English according to the guest's selected language. Show an unchecked acceptance box beside the terms. Enable **Confirm request** once the required information is valid and the guest checks the box.
3. The API checks the business, branch, current hours, blocked dates, slot interval, booking window, party size limits, other booking rules, and accepted terms. It saves one booking with status `requested` and returns its reference. A repeated submit or a retry after a lost response should return the same reference for the same submission. If the save fails, preserve the form and let the guest retry.
4. Show **Booking request received** with the reference, branch, arrival date and time, and party size. Tell the guest to expect a phone call after staff review availability. Staff receive a new booking alert in Telegram after the save succeeds. Retry temporary delivery failures and show the result in Super Control. The guest's booking reference stays valid.
5. Staff open **Admin Control → Bookings** to review the request in the list or see arrivals on the calendar. They open the same booking details from either view, check capacity for that arrival slot, and call the guest with their decision. An owner or manager records the outcome on the booking.

## Super Control settings

1. In **Business Detail → Feature Settings**, add a **Booking** switch for that business. Show whether its current plan includes Booking and which setting is preventing access when the feature is off.
2. In **Subscription Plans → Included Control features**, add a **Booking** checkbox. Store it with the existing plan features. Plans without Booking in their saved features and businesses without an explicit opt-in start with Booking off.
3. In **Business Detail → Booking Settings**, enable or disable bookings for individual branches and manage blocked dates, the arrival slot interval, minimum notice, advance booking limit, minimum and maximum party size, other booking rules, the business's Khmer and English terms, branch links, and Telegram alerts. Let Super Control prepare these settings before enabling the feature.

Both the assigned plan and the business switch must allow Booking. If either is off, the guest page shows booking unavailable and the public API rejects new requests. Preserve existing booking records and settings. Authorized staff retain read-only access to existing records while Booking is off. New requests also require an active branch with Booking enabled. Disabling a branch stops new requests for that branch and preserves its existing bookings for authorized staff.

Give booking alerts their own **Enable booking alerts** switch. Configure one **Booking bot token** per business and a **Telegram Chat ID** for each branch in **Booking Settings**. Use the dedicated booking bot to send to the saved destination for the booking's branch. If the token or branch Chat ID is missing, show that alerts need configuration and keep saving bookings. Store the token securely and return only whether it is configured. Let the operator select a branch, send a test message to its saved chat, and see the latest delivery result.

Each new booking alert includes the booking reference, business, branch, arrival date and time, and party size. Include the guest's name and phone number, plus the selected preset, free text preferences, and booking note when provided.

## Admin Control bookings

Add **Bookings** to the Admin Control sidebar at `/bookings` for business owners, managers, and staff with an explicit Booking grant. Enforce the same permission on direct routes and APIs and scope requests to the business from the signed-in session. When Booking is disabled, authorized users can still read existing bookings in the list, calendar, and details. The API blocks booking changes while disabled.

- **List.** Show booking reference, guest name, branch, arrival date and time, party size, and status. Staff can filter by branch and arrival date and open a booking from its row.
- **Calendar.** Open in month view and place bookings on their arrival date in the business time zone. Staff can navigate months, filter by branch, and open the booking details from an entry.
- **Details.** Show the booking reference, branch, arrival date and time, pax, guest contact, preset preference, preference text, note, status, submission time, and terms acceptance. Show **Confirm**, **Decline**, and **Cancel booking** actions to owners and managers when Booking is enabled and the action applies to the current status.

## Implementation sequence

1. **Prepare the pilot.** Identify the pilot plan, business, and branch. Gather the business's Khmer and English terms, the booking bot credentials, and the pilot branch's Telegram Chat ID for setup in Super Control.
2. **Add storage and APIs.** Add an additive D1 migration for `bookings`, settings, and booking alert delivery tracking. A booking stores its reference, business and branch IDs, arrival date and time with time zone, pax, preset preference, preference text, note, guest name and phone number, status, accepted terms version and time, and creation time. The public API resolves the business and branch from the menu link and returns only what guests need to submit. Use the existing protected Super Control Worker and its service binding for settings changes. Save the booking and queue its alert together when alerts are enabled.
3. **Wire up access and Super Control.** Add `bookings` to the shared Control page registry and plan feature editor. Save the business opt-in separately from sidebar visibility defaults. Add an explicit Booking access grant to staff membership settings. Apply both enablement checks to guest intake and keep the staff views read-only when either switch is off. Add the business switch and Booking Settings tab in `apps/super-control`.
4. **Build the guest and staff screens.** Add the mobile form to `apps/menu` with direct links on the custom and shared domains. Add the Bookings sidebar entry, list, calendar, and shared detail view with status actions to `apps/control`.
5. **Connect and verify Telegram.** Send the booking summary and guest details to the saved Chat ID for the booking's branch, track delivery, and use the existing retry pattern. Show the booking reference in every alert so staff can match it to the Admin Control record. Keep public booking success independent of the Telegram result.
6. **Verify and roll out.** Apply the additive migration before deploying the apps. Check both access switches, branch links, the guest form, the three Admin Control views, and Telegram. Enable Booking on the pilot plan, business, and branch, then verify a real booking end to end before wider rollout.

## API and storage design

Use the menu router's custom domain and platform route groups. Resolve a custom hostname with the existing public domain lookup and use the shared URL's business slug on the platform. Keep the booking view independent of the catalog loader so a business's menu or table selection rules do not block a booking link. Generate links from the business's current menu hostname or slug and its branch slug. Resolve historical business slugs through the existing alias lookup.

| Proposed endpoint                                               | Contract                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `GET /api/public/businesses/:slug/bookings`                     | With no `branch` query parameter, return the business's active branches that accept bookings. With `branch` set, return the resolved branch, public contact details, time zone, regular business hours, blocked dates, the slot interval, minimum notice, advance booking limit, party size limits, other arrival rules, preference choices, and the business's published Khmer and English terms and version. Return an unavailable result for a disabled or invalid booking link. |
| `POST /api/public/businesses/:slug/bookings?branch=:branch`     | Require an eligible branch in the query. Accept the arrival date, arrival time, pax, preset preference, preference text, note, guest name and phone number, terms acceptance, language, and version, and a submission ID. Resolve tenant ownership from the link and return only the receipt for that submission.                                                                                                                                                                   |
| `GET` and `PUT /api/super/businesses/:id/booking-feature`       | Read and update the business switch in Super Control. Return the saved switch, plan inclusion, and effective access.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| `GET` and `PUT /api/super/businesses/:id/booking-settings`      | Use the existing Access protected Super Control Worker and `SuperAdminService` to load and save branch participation, blocked dates, the slot interval, minimum notice, advance booking limit, party size limits, other rules, terms, and Telegram configuration including a dedicated booking bot token per business and a Chat ID per branch. Return a token presence flag and delivery summary.                                                                                  |
| `POST /api/super/businesses/:id/booking-settings/telegram-test` | Send a sample alert to the selected branch's saved Chat ID. Use sample text without guest information and return the result to the operator.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `GET /api/admin/bookings`                                       | Return bookings for the signed-in business and indicate whether Booking is in read-only mode. Use pagination for the list and an arrival date range for the calendar. Apply a branch filter when provided.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `GET /api/admin/bookings/:id`                                   | Return the full booking record for the signed-in business. Keep existing records readable when Booking is off and enforce staff permissions for every request.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `PATCH /api/admin/bookings/:id/status`                          | Let an owner or manager confirm, decline, or cancel a booking for the signed-in business while Booking is enabled. Validate the status change against the current record and return the saved status.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |

Scope `bookings` to both the business and branch and enforce ownership with a database constraint. Store the arrival as local date and time with its time zone and resolved UTC timestamp. Store the accepted terms language, immutable version, and server timestamp. If the terms change while the form is open, return the current version and clear the checkbox so the guest can review it.

Use a unique submission ID within the business and compare the normalized request on retry. An identical retry returns the original receipt. Reusing the ID with changed details returns a conflict. Insert the booking and its delivery record in one database operation. The Worker can attempt the alert after returning the receipt and use its existing scheduled Telegram retry path for transient failures. Give each delivery record the business and booking IDs so delivery status stays scoped to the selected business.

## Booking interview questions

The decisions above follow the numbering of these questions.

1. Should submitting create a request for staff to confirm, or immediately reserve a table?
2. Which guest contact details should be required, such as name and phone or name and email?
3. How should staff confirm or decline a booking with the guest, such as by phone, SMS, or Telegram?
4. If the booking link has no `branch`, should eMenu use the default branch, let the guest choose, or require a branch link?
5. What should **Booking preference** offer, such as indoor, outdoor, private room, free text, or a mix?
6. Should bookings follow business hours or have a separate schedule with dates staff can block?
7. Should guests pick a fixed arrival slot, such as every 30 minutes, or enter any allowed time?
8. What is the booking window, such as at least two hours' notice and up to 30 days ahead?
9. What minimum and maximum party sizes should the form accept?
10. Should staff decide capacity when they review requests, or should eMenu stop accepting requests when a time slot fills up?
11. Who should supply the terms, eMenu, the business, or each branch, and which languages should the form and terms support?
12. Should both the subscription plan and the business switch allow Booking, or may Super Control enable it for a business whose plan excludes it?
13. When Booking is disabled, should staff keep read access to previous bookings, or should the whole Bookings page be unavailable until re-enabled?
14. Should all active branches accept bookings by default when the business enables Booking, or should Super Control select branches individually?
15. Who should be able to see bookings and guest contact details in Admin Control, such as owners and managers only or additional staff roles?
16. Should the first Admin Control release let staff confirm, decline, or cancel bookings, or provide the list, calendar, and details for viewing?
17. Which calendar view should open first, day, week, or month?
18. Should all branches send alerts to one business Telegram chat, or should each branch use its own chat?
19. Should Booking use the existing order bot or have a separate bot token?
20. Should Telegram show the guest's contact details and note, or should staff open the booking in Admin Control for those details?
